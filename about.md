# About Me

Hello! I'm Grace Teng, aka pelicularities on the Internet. By day, I am a software engineer. I used to be an English teacher, and before that, I used to be a film student. These days, I create and teach in other ways.

This is my digital home base, a space where I share the things I love or am learning about. I call this space "eclectic commentary" for a reason: I have a wide range of interests, and enjoy synthesising my knowledge across multiple domains.

My great loves in life are video games, musical theatre and languages. I love getting lost in different worlds – whether travelling in different cities and cultures, hiking in the forest or in the mountains, or disappearing into role-playing games.

I currently live in [Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore), where I was born and raised. I went to college in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City) and spent a semester in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid), and love both cities still. I’ve also spent a lot of time in [Freiburg im Breisgau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freiburg_im_Breisgau), and have many happy memories of the city and the region. Someday, I hope to live and make my life in [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver), where my husband lives.

## Software Engineering
In mid-2020, in the thick of the [Covid pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic), I decided I wanted to become a software engineer. I completed [Le Wagon's](https://www.lewagon.com/) web development bootcamp, and shortly after took on a traineeship position at [Thoughtworks](https://www.thoughtworks.com/), which then turned into my first full-time software engineering role. I've since moved on from Thoughtworks, and now work at an investment bank on [software development lifecycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_development_life_cycle) tooling.

Most of my software engineering career so far has been characterised by boots-on-the-ground [legacy modernisation](https://martinfowler.com/tags/legacy%20modernization.html) work: taking an existing software system that still works but that has become a strategic liability, and re-tooling it to fit the organisation's ongoing tech strategy.

I'm very interested in computer systems, and enjoy into the nitty-gritty of how to make bits do cool things. One of the things I want to do on this site is to articulate my thoughts on building software, from my vantage point as a mid-level software engineer who's still honing her craft.

## Education
I graduated from the [University of Pennsylvania's](https://www.upenn.edu/) online [Masters of Computer and Information Technology](https://online.seas.upenn.edu/degrees/mcit-online/) program, which I completed part-time from Spring 2021 to Summer 2024. Before that, I studied film and television production, Spanish, and linguistics at [New York University](https://www.nyu.edu/).
